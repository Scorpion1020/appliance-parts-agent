## When to replace the water filter
Whirlpool recommends replacing refrigerator water filter cartridges approximately every six months, or sooner if water dispenser flow slows noticeably or ice develops off-tastes. Filter life varies with local water quality and usage volume.

## Flushing air after replacement
After installing a new filter cartridge, dispense and discard several gallons of water in short intervals to purge trapped air and loose carbon fines from the lines. Failure to flush can cause sputtering at the dispenser or cloudy initial ice cubes.
