## Condenser cleaning eligibility
Whirlpool directs owners to the model's manual before cleaning refrigerator condenser coils. Some models do not need condenser cleaning, while some require a certified technician. Dusty conditions or pets can change maintenance needs; do not impose one cleaning schedule on every refrigerator.

## Condenser cleaning precautions
For an owner-serviceable condenser, Whirlpool describes a vacuum with a soft brush for accessible surfaces and replacing the grille before restoring power. Isolate electrical power first, protect water connections, and avoid bending tubing or fins. A condenser-maintenance note is not evidence that a cooling fault has been diagnosed.
