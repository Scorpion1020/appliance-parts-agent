## Drainage obstructions
Excess standing water can result from obstructions near the sump, filters, drain hose, sink drain, or air gap. GE distinguishes normal residual water by filter design, so standing water alone does not establish a broken pump. Check the model's instructions before deciding which filters are removable.

## Drainage after installation
After a disposer replacement, an unremoved dishwasher connection plug can prevent drainage. A kinked hose or incorrect drain-hose routing can also matter. The required high-loop height comes from the dishwasher installation instructions, not a universal measurement. Excess detergent suds can interfere with drainage. Seek service if the checks do not resolve the problem.
