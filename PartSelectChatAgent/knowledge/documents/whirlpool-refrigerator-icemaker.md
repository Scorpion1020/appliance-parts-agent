## Ice maker power and freezer temperature
An automatic ice maker requires the freezer compartment to stay near 0 degrees Fahrenheit (-18 degrees Celsius) before it will cycle. Verify the wire shutoff arm or electronic on/off switch is enabled. Allow up to 24 hours after installation or temperature adjustment for full ice production.

## Water supply and filter checks
Low water pressure, a kinked supply line, or a clogged water filter can produce hollow cubes or stop ice production altogether. Confirm the household shutoff valve is fully open and replace overdue water filters before assuming the ice maker module or inlet valve has failed.
