# Operating sounds

Clicks can accompany temperature control or defrost switching. Refrigerant circulation can produce gurgling, while defrosting can cause dripping and some popping sounds. Ice falling into the bin also creates noise. These examples do not prove that every similar noise is harmless.

# Ice maker without water

An enabled ice maker without a water supply can rattle or vibrate; the manufacturer recommends switching it off when no water is connected. Other unexplained sounds may need a service technician rather than automatic replacement-part advice.
