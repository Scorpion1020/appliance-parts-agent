## Common causes of excessive freezer frost
Heavy frost buildup on freezer walls or packages typically points to warm, humid room air entering the compartment. Inspect the door gasket for gaps, tears, or food packages preventing a full seal, and minimize prolonged door openings in humid weather.

## When defrost service is needed
Modern frost-free refrigerators run periodic automatic defrost cycles. If thick ice forms strictly on the rear evaporator cover and the fresh food section warms up while the freezer stays cold, the defrost system or evaporator fan requires qualified diagnosis rather than manual chipping with sharp tools.
