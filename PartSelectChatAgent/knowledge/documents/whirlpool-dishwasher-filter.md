## Removable filter cleaning
Whirlpool describes a removable upper filter assembly that can be lifted out after a quarter-turn release. Rinsing removes most soil; stubborn deposits may need a soft brush. Reinstall the filter after cleaning. The design must be identified before using this procedure.

## Maintenance-free filter design
A different Whirlpool design uses a foreign-object cup below the lower rack and a filter system without routine filter cleaning. If the cup collects debris, it can be emptied and locked back into place. This distinction matters: a cleaning procedure for the removable-filter design should not be assumed to apply to every dishwasher.
