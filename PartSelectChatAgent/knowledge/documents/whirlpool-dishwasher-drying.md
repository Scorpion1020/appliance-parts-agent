## Rinse aid and cycle selection for drying
Dishwashers rely on rinse aid to reduce water surface tension so droplets sheet off dishes during the final heated rinse. Plastic items retain less heat than ceramic or glass and commonly hold moisture even when the heating system operates normally.

## Loading and vent clearance
Nesting bowls, overturned cup bottoms, or items blocking the door vent can trap steam inside the tub. Confirm the Heated Dry or Sanitize option is selected and allow the door to remain closed briefly after cycle completion before unloading from the bottom rack up.
