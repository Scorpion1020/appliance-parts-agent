# Check obstructions

Food packages, unseated drawers, items leaning out of door shelves, or a small ice cube can prevent complete closure. Rearrange obstructing items and close the bins. Some newer side-by-side models may still have shipping foam near the door bottom.

# Leveling and damage

Incorrect leveling can prevent self-closing; consult the installation instructions for the exact appliance. A broken hinge requires a technician. Failure to close alone does not establish which replacement part is needed.
