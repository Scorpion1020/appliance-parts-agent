## Eliminating dishwasher odor
Lingering odors usually stem from trapped food debris in the sump filter, standing water in a kinked drain hose, or infrequent high-temperature wash cycles. Rinse removable filters under warm water and wipe the door perimeter gasket where residue collects.

## Periodic tub cleaning
Running an empty hot cycle with an approved dishwasher cleaner helps dissolve grease and mineral scale from the tub interior and spray arms. Avoid mixing household bleach with acidic cleaners or using liquid dish soap, which creates excessive suds and leaks.
