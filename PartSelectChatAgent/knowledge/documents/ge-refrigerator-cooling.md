# Temperature and airflow

Check the temperature setting using the appliance manual: numbered dials and actual-temperature displays use different adjustment directions. Recently installed units can need about 24 hours to stabilize. Large containers can obstruct cold-air outlets; check that doors close and gaskets seal.

# Model-specific maintenance

Do not assume every refrigerator has user-cleanable condenser coils. Many models do not require this cleaning; follow the exact manual. Installation clearance and room conditions also affect cooling. Persistent warmth after basic checks warrants service, not an unsupported recommendation to replace a compressor.
