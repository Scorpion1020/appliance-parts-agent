## Checking spray arms and wash performance
Cloudy glassware or food particles left on dishes can result from blocked spray arm nozzles, overcrowded racks obstructing arm rotation, or incoming hot water that is below 120 degrees Fahrenheit. Spin each spray arm by hand before starting a cycle to confirm clearance.

## Detergent freshness and water hardness
Old or clumped detergent loses cleaning effectiveness. In hard-water areas, mineral film can build up on dishes and interior surfaces; using fresh detergent tablets paired with rinse aid improves wash clarity without replacing mechanical parts.
