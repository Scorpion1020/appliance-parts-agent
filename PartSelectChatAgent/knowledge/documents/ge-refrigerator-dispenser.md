## Slow or no water dispenser flow
A slow GE refrigerator water dispenser is frequently linked to a clogged water filter, a partially closed household supply valve, or a frozen water reservoir line inside the fresh food compartment. Verify dispenser lock is off and test flow after installing the bypass plug if provided.

## Temperature settings and frozen lines
Setting the fresh food compartment near freezing can ice up the internal water tank or door tubing. Raising the fresh food setting slightly toward 37 degrees Fahrenheit (3 degrees Celsius) helps prevent recurring line freeze-ups.
