## Checking for door leaks and oversudsing
Water pooling at the front of a GE dishwasher is often caused by excess suds from wrong detergent type, unlevel installation tilting the tub forward, or debris along the door seal. Wipe the tub lip and gasket clean and confirm the unit sits level side-to-side and front-to-back.

## Inspecting hoses and connections
If water appears underneath the unit rather than along the door edge, shut off power and water supply and inspect the inlet valve fitting and discharge hose clamp for looseness. Persistent under-tub leaks require service to avoid floor and electrical hazards.
